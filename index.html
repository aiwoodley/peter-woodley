DigiLabs TechTank · For schools & programs

Give your students a real path into tech

TechTank is eight online courses for grades 3–12. It covers teardowns, data security, AI infrastructure, Raspberry Pi, and repair and resale, all built on the same retired enterprise hardware that DigiLabs processes every day. Students start on a phone or a school Chromebook, with no lab and no kit required, and they finish with a portfolio of documented work.

  • School plans include teacher logins, class rosters, student progress reporting and completion certificates
  • Hands-on certification programs with middle schoolers, including at KIPP Miami
  • Built as a first step toward industry certifications, so students learn the vocabulary before the exam

How I work

Learn to understand. Teach to be understood.

  1. 01

    Learn

    I start hands-on. At DigiLabs that means real retired hardware: taking it apart, seeing what's inside, and learning how to destroy data the right way.

  2. 02

    Understand

    Knowing a fact isn't the same as understanding it. TechTank is built so the understanding comes first, before anyone sits for a certification.

  3. 03

    Teach

    Then I hand it to the next person, whether that's a middle schooler earning a certification, a client learning their new network, or a teammate using a tool I built.
