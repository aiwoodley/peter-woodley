01 / My story

West Palm Beach → FSU → Miami.

I grew up in West Palm Beach. I went to Florida State and left with a Master's in Product Development from the Jim Moran College of Entrepreneurship.

Miami is where it came together. In 2021 my twin brother Paul and I started DigiLabs, taking retired technology and turning it into a classroom. In 2022 I started Reflex Sports, a sports media and brand-building company.

Through all of it I've stayed grounded in my faith. "Humble yourself, He will exalt you." I try to live it every day: work hard, stay humble, and bring people with me.

02 / Philosophy

One sentence runs everything I do.

01

Learn

Everything starts with getting my hands on it. At DigiLabs that means real retired enterprise hardware: taking it apart, learning what's inside, and learning how to destroy data the right way under NIST 800-88.

02

Understand

Knowing a fact isn't the same as understanding it. TechTank turns that hardware into 8 online courses for grades 3–12 on teardowns, data security, AI infrastructure and repair/resale, so understanding comes before any certification.

03

Teach

Then you hand it to the next person. I run technology certification programs with middle schoolers at schools like KIPP Miami, and I've presented at Miami tech and education events.
